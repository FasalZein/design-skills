# DESIGN: Harbor marketing page (public landing page)
Direction: A "Board", dense and concrete. The dock-office door board becomes the page. Every tile is one door, one truck, one figure or one photo of the dock's world. The page's one memorable choice is the Docks board in #demo: an animated illustration in which the dispatcher sends a waiting truck to a free door and the board answers.

Tokens:
- Color (brand roles, light; map the shadcn roles in src/index.css onto these): ground #e6e5e1 (page); surface #ffffff (tiles, board, table rows); raised #f2f1ee (tile tone step, zebra rows, the rail's group rows in #demo); line #d3d1cb (tile tone, the edges of the #demo board and rail); rule #e6e5e1; control #8d8a82 (input and secondary-button borders); track #e6e5e1; text #141414; muted #55534e; shell #141414 (hero panel, dark tiles, wordmark tile, flanking CTA tile); shell-text #f3f2ef; shell-muted #b9b6ae; signal #f05a00 (primary buttons, the CTA tile, the footer link tile, the Now line, unloading bars, at-gate badge fill); signal-hover #ff6a12 (hover of every signal fill); shell-raised #2b2b2b (hover of ink buttons); on-signal #141414; signal-text #b84300 (the "Now 07:55" label); signal-pale #ffe3d1 (the recommended Network column in pricing); neg #c8281e, neg-text #a3201a, neg-pale #fde4e1, neg-ink #8a1b15 (delayed marks and badge); done-ink #3a3935.
- Orange rule on this page: a signal fill marks something to click or an active mark. Two fields are allowed because each is one whole link: the CTA tile and the footer "Book a demo" tile.
- Radius: 0 on every tile, photo, button, input, badge and bar.
- Elevation: none. No shadow on any resting element; tiles separate by an 8px gap and a tone step, never by a line. Lines appear only inside real tables, forms and data-ui fragments.
- Spacing (4px grid): tile gap 8 (12 in the hero sheet); tile inset 20 (16 on phones); board outer margin 12 (8 on phones); page margin 32 desktop, 20 phone; section gap 120 desktop, 88 phone; text-to-control 16–24. Round every prototype value that is off the 4px grid (6, 10, 14, 18, 22) to the nearest multiple of 4.
- Motion: one moving element, the #demo illustration (Components, #demo illustration). Nothing else on the page moves: no entrance animations, no scroll scenes, no transitions on hover or focus. Under prefers-reduced-motion the illustration shows its poster as a still, so nothing on the page moves.

- Tabular figures on every number. Sentence case everywhere; no uppercase labels, no eyebrows, no index numbers. h1–h3 use text-wrap: balance. Money and figures never truncate: they fit their tile or wrap.

- #demo script, played once (times from the moment the stage is in view): 0–1.4 s the 07:55 board holds (IN-20434 outlined at the gate at 08:45 on lane 01, door 01 "Free until 08:45"). Wide: the kit's real cursor travels from (1000, 380) to the primary, aims at 86% across (off its words) and presses. On the press: the IN-20434 bar moves to 07:55 (transform, 200 ms, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1), the app's own bar motion) and fills signal; door 01 rolls to "Unloading · 41 min left" (kit Roll); the rail's "At the gate" group closes (kit Collapse) and the rows below follow; the button label crossfades to "Undo: IN-20434 back to the gate". The cursor moves off to empty board at (900, 250) over 900 ms, then the result holds for 2.4 s or more and stays (about 7 s in all). Compact: the kit's touch dot taps the button at 88% across (no travel); door 01's state rolls, its slot time rolls 08:45 to 07:55, its badge crossfades from At gate to Unloading, the button crossfades to Undo; the result holds and stays (about 5.6 s). The cursor never shows in the poster.
- #demo Stop/Replay: the page's secondary button (48px, surface fill, 1px control border) at the right of the "Bar length" row. "Stop" while the scene plays; it shows the poster at once. "Replay" after the scene ends or stops; it remounts the stage at the story boundary (the kit's Explicit replay). Hidden under prefers-reduced-motion.
- Badges, door list and table rows: the app's components (StatusBadge B3, door list, 56px rows) inside data-ui.
- Pricing table: a real table element with scope attributes.
