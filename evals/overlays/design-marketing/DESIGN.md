# DESIGN: Harbor landing page (public-page, with an illustration slot)

Tree: Product / structural archetype -> one scrolling page: hero with the demo, features, pricing, FAQ, footer call to action (fixture, 2026-11-03)
Tree: Product / visual density -> airy page, dense product fragments (fixture, 2026-11-03)
Tree: Product / aesthetic direction -> calm, exact (fixture, 2026-11-03)
Tree: Product / unforgettable choice -> the demo timetable in the hero, times in IBM Plex Mono (fixture, 2026-11-03)

Tree: Section / Footer call to action -> one emphasis panel in --muted with the headline, one action and the company line (fixture, 2026-11-03)
Tokens: Write all tokens in src/index.css as CSS variables on :root, mapped through the existing `@theme inline` block. Light mode only: remove the .dark block. Component code uses semantic tokens only.
Tokens: Neutrals (hue 250): --background oklch(0.985 0.004 250); --foreground oklch(0.22 0.02 250); --card oklch(0.995 0.002 250); --card-foreground = --foreground; --popover = --card; --popover-foreground = --foreground; --muted oklch(0.955 0.008 250); --muted-foreground oklch(0.50 0.02 250); --accent = --muted; --accent-foreground = --foreground; --secondary = --muted; --secondary-foreground = --foreground; --border oklch(0.90 0.01 250); --input = --border.
Tokens: Action: --primary oklch(0.42 0.11 245); --primary-foreground oklch(0.985 0.004 250); --ring = --primary; --destructive oklch(0.55 0.19 27).
Tokens: Status inside the demo only: delayed oklch(0.50 0.16 40) on oklch(0.95 0.04 40), exposed as Tailwind colors status-delayed and status-delayed-tint; received oklch(0.47 0.11 150) on oklch(0.95 0.04 150), as status-received and status-received-tint.
Tokens: Radius --radius 0.375rem (6px): cards rounded-lg; buttons rounded-md; badges rounded-sm.
Tokens: Spacing: the Tailwind 4px scale. Section padding 96px top and bottom (py-24) at 1024px and up, 64px (py-16) below; page gutter 24px; content width 1152px (max-w-6xl); gap between grid cells 24px.
Tokens: Elevation: cards have a 1px --border hairline and no shadow at rest. No full-width rules between sections.
Tokens: Motion: --motion-fast 120ms (hover, press, color changes); --motion-base 200ms (content that enters); --motion-ease cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1). Map them to Tailwind's default transition duration and timing function. No section entrances. Under prefers-reduced-motion the demo shows its poster frame and nothing else moves.
Type: Faces: --font-sans 'IBM Plex Sans Variable' (package @fontsource-variable/ibm-plex-sans, OFL-1.1); --font-mono 'IBM Plex Mono' 400 and 500 (package @fontsource/ibm-plex-mono, OFL-1.1); both are in package.json. Remove the Geist import. Fallbacks: system-ui for sans, ui-monospace for mono.
Type: Scale: 14/20 (micro-labels, notes), 16/26 (body), 18/28 (lead line, card titles), 24/32 (section headings, weight 600), 48/52 (hero headline at 1024px and up, weight 600; 36/40 below), 40/44 (prices, mono, weight 500). Weights 400, 500, 600 only.
Type: Tracking: hero headline -0.02em; section headings -0.01em; body 0. Prices, times, pallet counts and IDs use the mono face with tabular-nums.
Layout: Header: 64px tall, the wordmark "Harbor" on the left, the links Features, Pricing and FAQ on the right; it scrolls away with the page.
Layout: Hero: two columns at 1024px and up, 5fr and 7fr, gap 48px, top-aligned: left the headline, the lead line and the two actions; right the product demo slot. Below 1024px: one column, the demo under the actions. Headline and lead are left-aligned.
Layout: Features, Pricing and FAQ: section heading left-aligned, above its content. Features: two columns at 768px and up, one below. Pricing: three columns at 1024px and up, one below.
Layout: Breakpoints: designed for 1280 to 1920px; checked at 1440, 1024 and 375. At 375px nothing overflows the viewport.
Components: Use the installed shadcn components (button, card, badge, separator) and lucide-react icons. Add no UI library.
Components: PricingCard (src/components/pricing-card.tsx): props { plan: Plan } (type in src/data/landing.ts). Order: plan name (18/28, weight 600), summary (16/26, --muted-foreground), price (40/44 mono; "Custom" when price is null) with the unit on the next line (14/20, --muted-foreground), the includes list (16/26, a 16px lucide Check in --primary before each item), then the action button full width. The featured plan has a 1px --primary border and the label "Most popular" as a badge after the name; the others a 1px --border. Card padding 24px, gap inside 16px. Prices and plan names never wrap mid-word or truncate.
Components: Pricing section (src/sections/pricing.tsx): the heading "Pricing", the line "Prices per month, excluding VAT. Cancel at the end of any month.", then one PricingCard per plan, equal height, actions aligned to the card bottom.
