## Decisions

The page, its sections and their order are the design tree's upper levels. A section the brief or DESIGN.md does not settle is open: attended, it joins the frontier; headless, it is an open part. A product demo or mock UI in a section is the illustration surface: read [illustration.md](illustration.md).

## Rules

- The house rules win over the craft floor on this surface for shadows at rest, eyebrows, monospace, thin rules, section backgrounds and section entrances.
- The craft floor's tracking floor holds for display type too, where "Laws of the craft" allows -0.05em: the design-qa checks fail anything tighter than the floor.
- App fragments on the page follow the taste skill's app-screen rules inside the fragment.
